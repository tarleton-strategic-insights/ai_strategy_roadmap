## Synopsis across all 7 statements

Despite different wording, the seven statements converge on a shared shape: **Tarleton
State will use AI, responsibly, to improve outcomes across learning/academics, workforce
readiness, and institutional operations — while protecting trust (integrity, privacy,
security).**

**Themes present across most statements:**

- **Student success** — present in 6 of 7 (VS-1, VS-2, VS-3, VS-5, VS-6, VS-7). The most
  consistent anchor across every draft.
- **Workforce readiness / development** — present in 5 of 7 (VS-2 via "life-long
  learning," VS-5, VS-6, VS-7, and implicitly VS-1's "work").
- **Operational effectiveness/efficiency** — present in 5 of 7 (VS-1, VS-2, VS-4, VS-5,
  VS-6).
- **Responsible/ethical/human-centered use of AI** — present in 5 of 7 (VS-3, VS-4, VS-5,
  VS-6, VS-7). Framed variously as "responsible and ethical," "human judgement,"
  "human-centered," or a named standard (integrity/privacy/trust).
- **Integrity, privacy, security, or trust** — present in 4 of 7 (VS-2, VS-4, VS-5, VS-6).
- **Research / innovation** — present in 3 of 7 (VS-4, VS-5, VS-7), least consistent of
  the major themes.

**Points of divergence:**

- **Scope of "who benefits."** Most versions center students; VS-3 and VS-6 explicitly
  widen scope to "stakeholders"/"colleague" (staff/faculty, not just students), and VS-7
  widens scope geographically ("Texas and beyond").
- **Tone/register.** VS-1, VS-2, VS-5 are plain and operational. VS-3 and VS-7 are more
  aspirational ("amplify human potential," "transformational impact... beyond!"). VS-4 and
  VS-6 read as the most formal/policy-like (quoted, standards-oriented).
- **Level of specificity.** VS-5 is the most exhaustive single-sentence list (six outcome
  areas + five protected values); VS-1 is the most compact.

**Working synthesis (not adjudicated):**

> Tarleton State University will responsibly use AI to improve student success, workforce
> readiness, and institutional operations, while protecting the integrity, privacy, and
> trust of our students, faculty, staff, and community.

This synthesis is a starting point for discussion, not a resolved decision — a formal
choice among (or synthesis of) these statements should go through the same adjudication
path as other framework-level items in `roadmap/OPEN_DECISIONS.md`.
