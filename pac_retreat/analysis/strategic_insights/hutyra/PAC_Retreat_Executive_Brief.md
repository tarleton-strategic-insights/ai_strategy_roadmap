# PAC Retreat: AI Roadmap Brief

The PAC retreat produced more than twenty AI project ideas. They sort into **three buckets by the resources they require** — not by theme — so leadership can allocate resources bucket by bucket instead of project by project.

| | **1: Workforce Upskilling** | **2: High-Technical Projects** | **3: Curriculum Integration** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **People** | Many | Few | Many |
| **Skill needed** | Shallow AI literacy | Deep engineering | Deep subject expertise, low AI skill |
| **Main burden** | Coordination & consistency | Build & delivery | Cross-department coordination |
| **Owner / SI role** | Org lead (TBD) / advisory | **SI / owner** | Academic Affairs (TBD) / advisory |

**Bucket 1** trains as much of the workforce as possible to practical AI proficiency — an organizational program, not a technical one. **Bucket 2** holds the complex builds, led by the AI teammate system (with interdepartmental awareness — ending the conflicting answers students get across offices), plus enrollment forecasting, early alerts, grant automation, and similar. **Bucket 3** embeds AI into curricula across every discipline, driven by faculty expertise and curriculum governance.

## Key findings

- **Last-mile delivery is the critical gap.** The university can build working prototypes but has no MLOps/DevOps capability to turn them into reliable, secure, user-facing tools. Nearly every project depends on it; today the burden falls on scarce senior data-science staff. This is the top hiring priority.
- **SI's leverage is Bucket 2 only.** Its rare technical skill set should not be spent on the wide-coordination work of Buckets 1 and 3; there, SI advises.
- **Micro-credentialing** (proposed at the retreat by President Hurley) spans Buckets 1 and 3 and points outward: paid AI credential paths for regional learners who want a real instructor, not another online course — community impact plus revenue.

## Decisions requested

1. Adopt the three-bucket framework for roadmap and budget planning.
2. Send the bucketed portfolio back to PAC for a **priority vote** — the immediate next step.
3. Confirm SI as owner of Bucket 2; advisory elsewhere.
4. Approve **MLOps/DevOps recruitment** to close the last-mile gap.
5. Name owners for Buckets 1 and 3.
6. Charter the **micro-credentialing design group** under President Hurley's sponsorship.

The funding logic throughout: new people for new tasks — tasks that, done well, reduce the need for additional people everywhere else. With the vote results and owners in place, the next iteration sequences projects and sets 90-day milestones.
