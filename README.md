# AI Roadmap — Command Center

Working repository for Tarleton's AI strategy & roadmap.

## How to use

Source material from the 2026-07-27 PAC retreat and prioritization survey lives in [supporting_materials](supporting_materials) and is progressively distilled into a working draft AI strategy and roadmap as follows (most distilled on top -> raw sources on bottom)

1. **[`AI_strategy_and_roadmap.md`](AI_strategy_and_roadmap.md)** (in development) — working draft of AI strategy and roadmap
1. **[`survey_analysis.md`](survey_analysis.md)** (complete) — analyzes [`survey_results.csv`](supporting_materials/prioritization/survey_results.csv)
1. **[`pac_report.md`](pac_report.md)**  (complete) — summarizes PAC retreat, combining [`use_cases_analysis.md`](supporting_materials/pac_retreat/analysis/use_cases_analysis.md) and [`vision_statements_analysis.md`](supporting_materials/pac_retreat/analysis/vision_statements_analysis.md) into a single report
1. **[`vision_statements_analysis.md`](supporting_materials/pac_retreat/analysis/vision_statements_analysis.md)** (complete) — every vision statement from PAC retreat synthesized into a final combined version
1. **[`use_cases_analysis.md`](supporting_materials/pac_retreat/analysis/use_cases_analysis.md)** (complete) — every use case from PAc retreat, deduplicated and categorized by outcome and by resources required as defined [here](supporting_materials/pac_retreat/analysis/strategic_insights/cook/use_cases)
1. **[`PAC source material`](supporting_materials/pac_retreat)** (complete) — raw materials from PAC retreat materials: flip-chart photos, workshop slides, pre-event case-study research, etc.
1. **[`Survey source material`](supporting_materials/prioritization)** (complete) — raw survey and results
