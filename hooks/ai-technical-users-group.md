# Hook — AI Technical Users Group

**Parked. Develop later.**

## The idea
A group to align people across the university doing technically advanced AI work.
Premise: everyone sees only pieces; the group assembles the full picture together.

## Open question to resolve first
Is the group's job to **align work already happening** (a coordination forum for known
players) or to **surface work nobody knows is happening** (a discovery mechanism that
must reach people who don't yet self-identify as doing advanced AI work)? The "everyone
sees pieces" framing points at the second — the harder, higher-value version, which
needs different membership than a standing coordination committee.

## Why it belongs in this repo
Likely a Capabilities/Foundation or Workforce-adjacent structure, and a plausible engine
for keeping OPEN_DECISIONS and roadmap sequencing honest (the people who'd catch a
mis-scoped Bucket 2 project early).
