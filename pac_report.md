## Introduction

This report synthesizes the outcomes of Tarleton State's PAC AI strategy retreat (2026-07-27) into a single reference: our working vision for AI at the university, and the concrete use cases surfaced at the retreat, organized by the strategic outcome each serves and the resources needed to deliver it.

This report was created by a human+AI team.

---

## Vision Statement

> Tarleton State University will responsibly harness AI to amplify student success, workforce readiness, and institutional impact — empowering our students, faculty, staff, and community to lead with integrity, privacy, and trust in an AI-enabled world.

See [vision_statements_analysis.md](pac_retreat/analysis/vision_statements_analysis.md) for details.
